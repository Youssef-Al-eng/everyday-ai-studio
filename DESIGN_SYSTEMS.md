# Seven product design systems

Seven maintained interface families organize 20 specialist workspaces. Each family changes layout, navigation, content hierarchy, and interaction structure; app-specific titles, task inputs, sidebars, tools, demo workflows, and output types remain individual.

## 1. Research & Editorial

**Purpose:** Researchers and analysts turning a question into an evidence-conscious brief.
**Agents (4):** Research Assistant, News Briefing Assistant, Market Research Comparator, Pros and Cons Reviewer
**Layout and hierarchy:** Narrow research-plan margin beside an editorial input and report canvas; citations/source notes stay visible.
**Tokens and typography:** Parchment #F5F1E9, ink #27362E, rust #B75C38, moss #75816B; Georgia-style evidence typography, 12px body, 1px warm borders, 3px corners.
**Shared components:** Question framing, plan steps, long-form report, source/limitation notes.
**Responsive behavior and accessibility:** Plan becomes a compact top rail; report and input stack on mobile. Semantic headings/forms, visible focus, explicit status text; don't rely on color alone.
**Rationale:** mirrors the established patterns users expect from editorial research, IDEs, analytics, queues, document readers, guided creation, and alert consoles. Compositions and app-specific workflow labels are original.
